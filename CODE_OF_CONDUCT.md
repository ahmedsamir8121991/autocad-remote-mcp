# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and helpful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about drawings, AutoCAD and the app. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, discussions or pull requests.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/ahmedsamir8121991/autocad-remote-mcp/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@ahmedsamir8121991](https://github.com/ahmedsamir8121991). Reports are handled confidentially.
